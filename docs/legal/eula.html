7. Activation, the trial and your data

The trial. The first time you open GoaSynth it starts a 24-hour trial covering every feature — the full sound engine, both filters, the effects, both sequencers, the AI patch designer and the factory bank. The start time is recorded in a small file in the shared public documents folder so that it cannot be reset by creating another user account on the same computer. When the 24 hours are up, instances render silence until they are activated; a valid serial (or our master key) supersedes the trial entirely.

Activation is offline. GoaSynth does not check a licence over the internet, does not phone home, does not require an account and sends us nothing. What it needs is a serial — an RSA-signed statement of your computer's machine ID. That machine ID is shown on the activation screen and is a fingerprint of that machine, so a serial activates the machine it was issued for and no other, and a ledger file on the machine records which machine each serial belongs to, refusing one that is already bound elsewhere. There is no dongle, no login, no subscription check and no expiry: once activated, that build stays activated.

Keep your order email. Your serial, or the .goalicense file containing it, is part of your purchase. Losing it does not invalidate your licence — write to goasynth.support@gmail.com and we will re-issue it. Re-installing your operating system or changing hardware changes the machine ID, and in that case we release the old binding and issue a fresh serial free of charge. The plugin can also deactivate itself, which frees that machine's ledger entry.

What we ask of you. Do not share, publish or resell your serial; do not activate more machines than §5 allows; and do not attempt to defeat the activation or the trial, including editing the trial file or the ledger. Nothing here is used to monitor you: the plugin reports nothing, and the machine ID is the only machine information we ever receive, once, when your licence is issued.

Cloud AI is optional, and it is your own connection. The AI patch designer runs entirely offline by default. If you choose to switch it to a cloud engine — Gemini, an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or a base URL of your own — and supply your own API key, the plugin sends the prompt you typed directly to that provider, under your account and their terms. We are not a party to that, receive nothing from it and cannot see your prompts. Your presets, drawn wavetables, project data and audio stay on your own machine either way.
